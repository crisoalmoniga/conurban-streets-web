The game

Conurban Streets is an arcade racer through the streets of Buenos Aires, from the city to its suburbs. You start out in everyday rides, beaten-up Argentine classics on good old wheels, and race by race you chase the grand prize: cars rebuilt with thrusters that hover over the asphalt. No chrome megacities here: just a future held together with wire and streets you can almost smell. Release is planned for 2027.

Good old rides

The Renault 12, the Fiat 147, the Peugeot 504 and the Chevrolet C10 pickup: classic Argentine cars, beaten up and rebuilt with whatever was lying around, each with its own handling. They all roll on good old wheels, until you earn the grand prize: the 12 on thrusters, hovering.

Places you know

Tracks inspired by all of Buenos Aires, on the streets you walk every day: from La Boca and the Autódromo to Escalada and the Mercado Central, in an improvised retro-futuristic key.

Short, chaotic races

Pick-up-and-play arcade handling, three difficulty levels, nitro for that last push, rivals that fight for every corner and walls that bite back.

Build your collection

Every race earns points and every circuit you win unlocks a new ride. Spend the points on engine, handling and nitro parts, then tune each car by hand in the workshop.

The street won't wait

City buses pulling over at every stop, traffic lights, trucks hauling trailers and rocks dropped from footbridges that crack your windshield.

Barrio humor

Street signs, murals and local landmarks full of nods for anyone who grew up on either side of the General Paz. With streets you already know and music by local urban artists from right around the corner.

The circuits

Remedios de Escalada

Next to the train line, among sculptures and neighborhood signs.

Autódromo del Parque

The speedway at night, with the city skyline behind.

Camino Negro

Narrow streets, the Riachuelo river, the Puente La Noria overpass and footbridges where someone's waiting with rocks.

La Boca and Dock Sud

Along the Riachuelo riverside, past the Usina del Arte and the iron bridges.

Mercado Central

Before dawn, between trucks and right through the market halls.

Menemista Unlockable

The prize for winning it all: the steep streets of a Rio favela, the tunnel under the hill and Christ the Redeemer watching from above.
